## 🎯 Practical Methodology & Analysis

To sharpen my analytical thinking and incident response skills, I leverage **Artificial Intelligence** to generate realistic simulated scenarios and study cases.

For each case documented in the initial files, I perform a thorough investigation and craft a **structured technical report** outlining:

* 🔍 **Incident Overview & Triage:** Exactly what happened and which assets were impacted.
* ⚠️ **Root Cause Analysis:** The underlying vulnerability or attack vector that led to the event.
* 🛡️ **Remediation & Mitigation:** Step-by-step resolution and long-term security recommendations.
* 📚 **Industry Frameworks:** Mapping actions and techniques to standard frameworks such as **NIST SP 800-61** and **MITRE ATT&CK**.
